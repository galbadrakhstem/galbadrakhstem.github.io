  <h2 id="mongol-khel">Монгол хэл</h2>
  <div>
    <p>Mongolian language — a grade 11 subject of the Mongolian national curriculum (<span lang="mn">бүрэн дунд боловсрол</span>). The textbook is organised into four chapters (the last is printed as VI in the textbook) covering descriptive writing, evidence and analysis, argumentation, and dictionaries.</p>

    <ul>

    <h3 id="how-to-prepare">How to prepare</h3>
    <p>Focus on structure: for each text type (travel note, scientific summary, reflective essay) note the purpose, audience, and typical paragraph plan. Practise identifying the grammar point of each chapter (conjoined, complex, mixed sentences) in your own writing.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Use the assessment criteria (PDF above) to see how content, language, and structure are scored.</li>
      <li>Keep a dictionary log: for <span lang="mn">Толь бичиг</span>, record the dictionary type, its purpose, and an example entry.</li>
      <li>Write one short piece per chapter and revise it for clarity, cohesion, and correct citation of sources.</li>
    </ul>

    <div class="notice">
      <strong>Edition:</strong> the national curriculum is revised from time to time. Check with your school which edition applies to your grade 11 year. Textbook page numbers are from the 11th grade <span lang="mn">Монгол хэл</span> textbook; the last chapter is printed as <span lang="mn">VI БҮЛЭГ</span> in the textbook.
