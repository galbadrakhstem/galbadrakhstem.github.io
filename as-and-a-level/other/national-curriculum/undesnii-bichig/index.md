    <h3 id="past-exam-walkthroughs">Past-exam walkthroughs</h3>
    <p>To be added!</p>

    <h3 id="how-to-prepare">How to prepare</h3>
    <p>Practise reading and writing the traditional script daily. Keep a notebook of new words in both Cyrillic and traditional script, and copy short texts to build fluency. Use the Ministry curriculum page above to see the learning outcomes for your grade.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Write each new character 10 times, then use it in a word and a short sentence.</li>
      <li>Read one paragraph of printed traditional script each day and transcribe it to Cyrillic, then back.</li>
      <li>When past-exam tasks are available, practise under timed conditions and check against the official criteria.</li>
    </ul>

    <div class="notice">
      <strong>Edition:</strong> the national curriculum is revised from time to time. Check with your school which edition applies to your grade 11 year.
