    <h3 id="how-to-prepare">How to prepare</h3>
    <p>For each work, note the author, genre, main characters, and one key theme. Practise summarising the plot in 3–4 sentences and explaining how a short excerpt illustrates the theme. Use the assessment criteria to see what counts as evidence and analysis.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Make a table of works by chapter with a one-line summary and a quotation you can analyse.</li>
      <li>For poetry (<span lang="mn">яруу найраг</span>), note form and imagery; for drama, note stage directions and conflict.</li>
      <li>Write past-exam answers with a clear structure — context, summary, analysis, personal response — and check against the criteria.</li>
    </ul>
