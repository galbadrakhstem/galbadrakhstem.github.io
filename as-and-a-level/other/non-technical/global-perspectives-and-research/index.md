# Global Perspectives and Research

<p class="lead">Cambridge International AS &amp; A Level Global Perspectives &amp; Research (9239) is a skills-based course: analysing arguments and perspectives on global issues, researching, working in a team project and, at A Level, writing an independent research report.</p>

## How it is assessed

<div class="table-wrap">
<table>
  <thead>
    <tr><th>Component</th><th>What you do</th><th>Marks</th><th>Weighting</th></tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>1 Written Exam</td><td>1 hour 30 minutes: three compulsory questions on source documents that present perspectives on an issue of global significance.</td><td>45</td><td>36% of AS · 18% of A Level</td></tr>
    <tr><td>2 Essay</td><td>An essay of 1750–2000 words on a global issue of your choice, with a title you devise yourself.</td><td>40</td><td>32% of AS · 16% of A Level</td></tr>
    <tr><td>3 Team Project</td><td>An individual presentation of your research and proposed solution (30 marks) and a reflective paper (10 marks).</td><td>40</td><td>32% of AS · 16% of A Level</td></tr>
    <tr><td>4 Cambridge Research Report</td><td>A research report of up to 5000 words on a question you devise (75 marks) and a research log (10 marks).</td><td>85</td><td>50% of A Level</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
</div>

Components 1, 2 and 3 make up the AS Level; Component 4 completes the A Level. All four are externally assessed.
