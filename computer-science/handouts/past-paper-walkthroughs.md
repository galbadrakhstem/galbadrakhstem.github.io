## What a walkthrough contains

<ul class="link-list">
  <li><strong>The paper and its mark scheme.</strong> Every component page links the official Cambridge question paper and mark scheme, and states the syllabus the paper was examined under.</li>
  <li><strong>Question-by-question working.</strong> Each part is answered in full — conversions written out, algorithms traced, and pseudocode written to the Cambridge conventions.</li>
  <li><strong>Where the marks are.</strong> The marking points in the mark scheme are matched to the part of the answer that earns them.</li>
  <li><strong>What the examiner saw.</strong> The comments from the examiner report for that series, applied to the question in front of you.</li>
</ul>

<div class="notice">
<strong>Syllabus versions:</strong> 9618 question papers are written to the syllabus in force in that examination series. This site cites the official syllabus documents — <a href="https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/636089-2024-2025-syllabus.pdf">2024–2025</a>, <a href="https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/697372-2026-syllabus.pdf">2026</a> and <a href="https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/721397-2027-2029-syllabus.pdf">2027–2029</a> — so you can check which content applies to the paper you are practising.
</div>
