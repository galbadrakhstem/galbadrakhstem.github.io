    <h3 id="how-to-use">How to use a chapter handout</h3>
    <ol>
      <li>Read the outcomes list and mark any you cannot yet do without notes.</li>
      <li>Work each example yourself before reading the next line of the solution.</li>
      <li>Attempt the self-assessment questions (SA) closed-book, then compare the complete method in SA Sol — the working, the trace, the pseudocode — not only the final answer.</li>
      <li>Do the exam-style questions (ES) and mark them with ES Sol, then finish with past-paper questions on the same section.</li>
