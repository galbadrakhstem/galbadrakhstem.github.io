## What you need to be able to do

- Compare RISC and CISC processors, including pipelining, the use of registers, and interrupt handling.
- Describe the SISD, SIMD, MISD and MIMD architectures and the characteristics of massively parallel computers.
- Explain the concept of a virtual machine, with examples of its role, benefits and limitations.
- Produce truth tables for logic circuits including half adders, full adders and SR and JK flip-flops, and explain flip-flops as data storage elements.
- Simplify logic expressions and circuits with Boolean algebra, De Morgan’s laws and Karnaugh maps.
