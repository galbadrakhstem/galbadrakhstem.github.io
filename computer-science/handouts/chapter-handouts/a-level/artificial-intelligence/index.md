## What you need to be able to do

- Explain how graphs are used in artificial intelligence, and use Dijkstra’s algorithm and the A* algorithm to search a graph.
- Explain how artificial neural networks have helped with machine learning.
- Distinguish machine learning, deep learning and reinforcement learning, and supervised and unsupervised learning, with reasons for using each.
- Explain back propagation of errors and regression methods in machine learning.
