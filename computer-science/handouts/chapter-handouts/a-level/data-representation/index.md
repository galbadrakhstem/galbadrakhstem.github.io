## What you need to be able to do

- Explain why user-defined data types are needed, and define and use non-composite types (enumerated, pointer) and composite types (set, record, class/object).
- Choose serial, sequential or random file organisation, and sequential or direct access, for a given problem.
- Describe and use hashing algorithms to read from and write to random files.
- Describe binary floating-point numbers with a two’s complement mantissa and exponent, convert between them and denary, and normalise them.
- Explain the effect of the split between mantissa and exponent, why a binary representation may only approximate a real number, and how rounding errors, overflow and underflow arise.
