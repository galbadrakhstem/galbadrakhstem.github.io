# AS Level — sections 1 to 12

<p class="lead">The AS Level content of 9618. Sections 1–8 are the theory examined in Paper 1; sections 9–12 are the problem-solving and programming examined in Paper 2.</p>

## How to use a chapter handout

1. Read the outcomes list and mark any you cannot yet do without notes.
2. Work each example yourself before reading the next line of the solution.
3. Attempt the practice questions closed-book, then compare the complete method — the working, the trace, the pseudocode — not only the final answer.
4. Finish with past-paper questions on the same section.
