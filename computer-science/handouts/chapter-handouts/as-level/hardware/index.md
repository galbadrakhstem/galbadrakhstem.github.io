## What you need to be able to do

- Explain the need for input, output, primary memory and secondary storage, and the benefits and drawbacks of embedded systems.
- Describe the operation of devices including laser and 3D printers, microphones, speakers, magnetic, solid-state and optical storage, touchscreens and virtual reality headsets, and the use of buffers.
- Compare RAM and ROM, static and dynamic RAM, and PROM, EPROM and EEPROM.
- Explain monitoring and control systems, including sensors, actuators and feedback.
- Use NOT, AND, OR, NAND, NOR and XOR gates, and move between problem statements, logic circuits, truth tables and logic expressions.
