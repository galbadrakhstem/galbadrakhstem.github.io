## What you need to be able to do

- Distinguish binary prefixes (kibi, mebi, gibi, tebi) from decimal prefixes (kilo, mega, giga, tera).
- Convert between binary, denary, hexadecimal, Binary Coded Decimal and one’s and two’s complement, and carry out binary addition and subtraction, including overflow.
- Describe practical uses of BCD and hexadecimal, and represent characters using ASCII, extended ASCII and Unicode.
- Explain how bitmap and vector images are encoded, estimate bitmap file sizes, and justify a bitmap or a vector graphic for a task.
- Explain how sound is sampled and the effect of sampling rate and resolution, and compare lossy and lossless compression, including run-length encoding.
