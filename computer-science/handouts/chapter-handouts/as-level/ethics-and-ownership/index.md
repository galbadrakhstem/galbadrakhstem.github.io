## What you need to be able to do

- Explain the need for ethics as a computing professional and the importance of professional bodies such as BCS and IEEE.
- Explain the impact of acting ethically or unethically in a given situation.
- Explain the need for copyright legislation.
- Compare types of software licence — Free Software Foundation, Open Source Initiative, shareware and commercial — and justify a licence for a situation.
- Explain the applications of artificial intelligence and its social, economic and environmental impact.
