## What you need to be able to do

- Explain the purpose of networking devices, LANs and WANs, the client–server and peer-to-peer models, and thin and thick clients.
- Describe bus, star, mesh and hybrid topologies, cloud computing, and the differences between wired and wireless media.
- Describe the hardware that supports a LAN, the role of a router, Ethernet, and how CSMA/CD detects and avoids collisions.
- Explain bit streaming, the difference between the World Wide Web and the internet, and the hardware that supports the internet.
- Explain IPv4 and IPv6 addresses, subnetting, public and private and static and dynamic addresses, and how a URL is resolved with the Domain Name Service.
