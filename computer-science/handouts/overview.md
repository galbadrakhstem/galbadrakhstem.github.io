# Overview

<p class="lead">Cambridge International AS &amp; A Level Computer Science is syllabus code 9618. It is examined in four papers: two make up the AS Level, and two more complete the A Level.</p>

## The four components

</div>

Papers 1 and 2 make up the AS Level. For the A Level you also sit Papers 3 and 4, either in the same series or in a later series with your AS Level result carried forward. Calculators must not be used in any paper.

## How to use these handouts

1. Read the overview of the paper you are sitting, then check which sections it covers.
2. Work through the chapter handouts in order; each one states the syllabus section it covers.
3. Use the step-by-step solutions to compare method — conversions, traces and pseudocode — not just answers.
4. Finish with past papers under timed conditions, then re-read the mark scheme.
