# Paper 3 — Advanced Theory

<p class="lead">A written paper on the A Level content, sections 13–20: data representation, protocols, processors and Boolean algebra, operating systems and translators, security, artificial intelligence, algorithms and programming paradigms — 75 marks in 1 hour 30 minutes.</p>
