# Frequently asked questions

<p class="lead">Short answers about how the Computer Science 9618 handouts are organised and how to use them.</p>

## What do Handout, SA, SA Sol, ES and ES Sol mean?

Every chapter has the same five links. **Handout** opens the chapter handout: the syllabus reference, what you need to be able to do, notes and worked examples. **SA** is a set of self-assessment questions and **SA Sol** their full solutions; **ES** is a set of exam-style questions and **ES Sol** their full solutions.

## Why are the chapters grouped by paper?

Each paper examines particular syllabus sections: Paper 1 (Theory Fundamentals) sections 1–8, Paper 2 (Fundamental Problem-solving and Programming Skills) sections 9–12, Paper 3 (Advanced Theory) sections 13–20, and Paper 4 (Practical) sections 19–20, except low-level and declarative programming. Sections 19 and 20 therefore appear under both Paper 3 and Paper 4.

## Which papers make up AS Level and A Level?

Papers 1 and 2 make up the AS Level. For the A Level you also sit Papers 3 and 4, either in the same series or in a later series with your AS Level result carried forward. The [Overview]({{ '/computer-science/handouts/overview/' | relative_url }}) lists the time, marks and weighting of each paper.

## Which syllabus do the handouts follow?

Every page cites the syllabus section it works from. Use the [2026 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/697372-2026-syllabus.pdf) for the 2026 series and the [2027–2029 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/721397-2027-2029-syllabus.pdf) for 2027, 2028 and 2029; the 20 sections and the four papers are the same in both. Always check the document for your own examination year.

## Can I use a calculator?

No. Calculators must not be used in any 9618 paper.

## Are these official Cambridge materials?

No. Galbadrakh STEM is an independent study site. Questions written for it are original practice questions, not past-paper questions, and the outcome lists on chapter pages are study paraphrases of the syllabus, not quotations. Official past papers and mark schemes are on the [Cambridge International 9618 page](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-international-as-and-a-level-computer-science-9618/).

## A link opens a section that says “To be added!”

The handouts and question sets are written one chapter at a time. A section that reads “To be added!” has not been written yet; it appears in the same place on every chapter page, so new material is easy to find.
