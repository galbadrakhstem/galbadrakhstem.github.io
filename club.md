  <div class="about-biography">
    <p>The Physics &amp; Astronomy Club at Mongol Aspiration International School. Its aim is to give students a starting point in astronomy, and a place where questions can be asked until they become understanding.</p>
    <h4 id="founded">Founded</h4>
    <p>On 14 November 2024, Galbadrakh Ulziibold founded the Physics &amp; Astronomy Club at Mongol Aspiration International School.</p>
    <h4 id="purpose">Purpose</h4>
    <p>The club gives students the starting point that is often missing. It is a community where curiosity is welcome, questions have room to develop, and a student who knows nothing about astrophysics can grow step by step toward something real.</p>
    <p>The aim is to help students see the world the way the founder now sees it: the sky is not just a backdrop, and the rules behind it can be learned.</p>
    <h4 id="teaching">Teaching</h4>
    <p>Orbital mechanics is the core topic. It was taught to club members throughout 9th and 10th grade, returning to the same material with new students, new questions and new gaps to bridge. Each repetition deepened the teacher's own understanding.</p>
    <p>A-level content is explained with intuitive methods for high-school students.</p>
    <p>In July 2026 the founder was invited to teach the astronomy component to students preparing for the International Earth Science Olympiad.</p>
    <p>Direct evidence: thanked by name on the IESO page of the Mongolian Geography Olympiad Committee (<a href="https://geography.mn/ieso#history">https://geography.mn/ieso#history</a>), among those who worked on the team's preparation for the 19th IESO (Turin, Italy, August 2026): "Монгол тэмүүлэл сургуулийн 11В ангийн сурагч Ө.Галбадрах нартаа талархал дэвшүүлье." ("We express our gratitude to … U. Galbadrakh, a class 11V student at Mongol Temuulel School.")</p>
    <h4 id="results">Results</h4>
    <ul>
      <li>2025–2026: Club prepared students for National Astronomy Olympiad — 4 medals middle level, 2 medals senior level.</li>
      <li>Member D. Dolgion — prize winner at International Astronomy Olympiad.</li>
      <li>Members have delivered research presentations, including one on orbital resonance.</li>
      <li>Six students from the school have won medals at the National Olympiad (2024–2025 cycle).</li>
    </ul>
    <h4 id="achievements">Founder achievements</h4>
    <ul>
      <li>OWAO 2025 — Open World Astronomy Olympiad: Gold Medal and Diamond award, listed in the <a href="https://owao.siriusolymp.ru/2025en/results">official OWAO 2025 list of the winners and prize-winners</a> (U. Galbadrakh, Mongolia — Gold).</li>
      <li>National Astronomy Olympiad IX — Gold Medal.</li>
      <li>National Astronomy Olympiad X — Bronze Medal, Senior Level.</li>
    </ul>
