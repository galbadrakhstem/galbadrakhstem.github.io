# About

## Who writes this

I'm Galbadrakh, a Grade 11 student in class 28C at Aspiration International School in Mongolia. I study Cambridge International AS &amp; A Level with a STEM-heavy timetable, and the subjects I spend most of my time on are mathematics and artificial intelligence.

I'm not a teacher, and there is no publisher behind this site. I'm a student who kept running into the same problem and decided to fix it properly instead of working around it.

## Why this site exists

When you finish a past paper, the usual routine is this: mark it, put the mark scheme away, and move on. But a mark scheme only tells you what counts as acceptable. It doesn't tell you where the answer came from. You can see `M1` and `A1` printed beside a line of working and still have no idea how anyone thought of that line.

That gap pushes students towards something that doesn't help them. They read an answer, don't understand it, paste the question into an AI, and copy the explanation into a notebook. The working ends up in the notebook and nowhere else. Coursebook answers have the same problem: even when you know the final answer, you often still don't know *how* to answer the question.

My own learning depends on seeing every step — the substitution written out, the arithmetic actually done, the reason one method was chosen over another, the exact point where a mark is earned, and why that approach is faster and more reliable. So that is what I write, for every question on this site.

Each solution is built to answer four things:

1. What is the examiner looking for on this question?
2. What information does the question actually give you?
3. Which technique does it expect, and why that one?
4. How do you get from the question to the answer — correctly, quickly and precisely?

## What you'll find here

- **Step-by-step solutions.** Self-assessment and exam-style questions solved line by line, with no intermediate step left out. Read the question here and follow the working here. Nothing needs to be pasted anywhere else, and nothing needs to be copied out of an AI answer.
- **Past-paper walkthroughs.** Each paper is identified by its code and series, for example 9709/13, May/June. I restate the question in my own words and then solve it in full, because the papers and mark schemes themselves belong to Cambridge International.
- **Chapter handouts.** One handout per coursebook chapter, so an entire chapter sits in a single place.
- **Last-minute prep.** A five-minute glance-through for each topic, for the morning of the examination.
- **Everything in one tab.** No dozen open browser windows, and no hunting for a second explanation when the first one assumed a step you never saw.

## Working smart

You can put in a huge amount of work and still not improve. Working smart is having good materials in front of you, so that when you do sit down to study, the only thing left to do is study. That's the point of this site.
