# Physics exam overview

Cambridge International AS &amp; A Level Physics (9702) assesses the understanding of physical concepts and their application in new situations, together with practical skills examined in a timetabled practical paper. This page is a study-planning guide; it is not a substitute for the syllabus for your examination year.

## Check your assessment route first

9702 has three routes: AS Level only (Papers 1, 2 and 3 in the same series), an A Level staged over two years (Papers 1, 2 and 3, then Papers 4 and 5 with the AS Level result carried forward), or the whole A Level in one series. Before you make a revision timetable:

1. Find the official 9702 syllabus for the year in which you will sit the examination.
2. Confirm with your teacher which route and which examination series you are entered for.
3. Make a checklist of the learning outcomes: topics 1–11 for AS Level, all 25 topics for A Level.
4. Check the examination timetable, including the date of the Paper 3 practical examination.

## What to practise

The syllabus is organised into 25 topics, with practical skills assessed separately in Papers 3 and 5. Across topics, practise the following examination habits:

- **Write the relationship first.** State the equation you are using before you substitute any values.
- **Work in SI units.** Convert before substituting, keep units attached to quantities, and check that the final unit matches the quantity you are finding.
- **Give a sensible precision.** Follow the question's instruction; when there is none, match the number of significant figures in the data.
- **Learn definitions exactly.** Many marks are for precise statements — for example, potential difference as the energy transferred per unit charge.
- **Know the data and formulae pages.** Papers 1 and 2 print the data and formulae on page 2 and Paper 4 on pages 2 and 3; any other relationship must be recalled.
- **Practise the practical conventions.** Tables, graphs, gradients and uncertainties are marked to the same conventions in Papers 3 and 5.
