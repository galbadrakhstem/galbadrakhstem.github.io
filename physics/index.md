    <h3 id="how-to-prepare">How to prepare</h3>
    <p>In calculations, write the governing relationship before substituting values. Convert to consistent SI units, keep units attached to quantities, and check that the final unit matches the quantity you are finding. State an answer to a sensible precision, following the question's instructions.</p>
