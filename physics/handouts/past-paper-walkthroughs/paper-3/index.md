## Skills in this component

The practical skills are set out in section 5 (Practical assessment) of the syllabus. In outline:

<ul>
  <li>Set up apparatus from written instructions and diagrams, and take measurements with suitable instruments to an appropriate precision.</li>
  <li>Record raw readings in a table whose column headings give each quantity and its unit, with consistent precision in each column.</li>
  <li>Plot a graph on a sensible scale, draw the line of best fit, and find its gradient and intercept.</li>
  <li>Estimate uncertainties, draw a conclusion from the data, and evaluate the procedure by identifying limitations and suggesting improvements.</li>
</ul>
