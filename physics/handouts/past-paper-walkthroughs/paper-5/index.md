## Skills in this component

The practical skills are set out in section 5 (Practical assessment) of the syllabus. In outline:

<ul>
  <li>Plan an experiment to test a given relationship: identify the independent and dependent variables, describe the method and measurements, and explain how the other variables are kept constant, with safety precautions.</li>
  <li>Choose what to plot to obtain a straight line, then plot the data with error bars and draw the line of best fit and the worst acceptable line.</li>
  <li>Find the gradient and intercept with their uncertainties, and use them to determine the quantities in the relationship.</li>
  <li>Draw conclusions from the analysis and evaluate whether the data support the suggested relationship.</li>
</ul>
