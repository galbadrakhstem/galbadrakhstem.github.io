## What a walkthrough contains

<ul class="link-list">
  <li><strong>The paper and its mark scheme.</strong> Every component page links the official Cambridge question paper and mark scheme, and states the syllabus the paper was examined under.</li>
  <li><strong>Question-by-question working.</strong> Each part is solved in full — the relationship stated, every substitution written with its unit, and the answer given to a sensible precision.</li>
  <li><strong>Where the marks are.</strong> The marks in the mark scheme are matched to the step of working that earns them.</li>
  <li><strong>What the examiner saw.</strong> The comments from the examiner report for that series, applied to the question in front of you.</li>
</ul>

<div class="notice">
<strong>Syllabus versions:</strong> 9702 question papers are written to the syllabus in force in that examination series. This site cites the official syllabus documents — <a href="https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/664565-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf">2025–2027</a> and <a href="https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/744626-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf">2028–2030</a> — so you can check which content applies to the paper you are practising. Papers from 2022 to 2024 were examined under the previous syllabus; Cambridge states that the 2025–2027 syllabus has no significant changes which affect teaching.
</div>
