# Overview

<p class="lead">Cambridge International AS &amp; A Level Physics is syllabus code 9702. It is examined in five papers: three make up the AS Level, and two more complete the A Level.</p>

Papers 1, 2 and 3 make up the AS Level. For the A Level you also sit Papers 4 and 5, either in the same series or in a later series with your AS Level result carried forward, so confirm your own route with your school before you plan revision.

## How to use these handouts

1. Read the overview of the paper you are sitting, then check which topics it covers.
2. Work through the chapter handouts in order; each one states the syllabus topic and sections it covers.
3. Use the step-by-step solutions to compare method, units and precision, not just answers.
4. Finish with past papers under timed conditions, then re-read the mark scheme.
