# Frequently asked questions

<p class="lead">Short answers about how the Physics 9702 handouts are organised and how to use them.</p>

## What do Handout, SA, SA Sol, ES and ES Sol mean?

Every chapter has the same five links. **Handout** opens the chapter handout: the syllabus reference, what you need to be able to do, notes and worked examples. **SA** is a set of self-assessment questions and **SA Sol** their full solutions; **ES** is a set of exam-style questions and **ES Sol** their full solutions.

## Why are the chapters split into AS Level and A Level?

That is how the syllabus divides the topics. Topics 1–11 are the AS Level content, examined in Paper 1 (Multiple Choice) and Paper 2 (AS Level Structured Questions); topics 12–25 are the A Level content, examined in Paper 4 (A Level Structured Questions) with the AS Level topics assumed. Paper 3 (Advanced Practical Skills) and Paper 5 (Planning, Analysis and Evaluation) are practical papers; their walkthrough pages outline the skills they test.

## Which papers make up AS Level and A Level?

Papers 1, 2 and 3 make up the AS Level. For the A Level you also sit Papers 4 and 5, either in the same series or in a later series with your AS Level result carried forward, so confirm your own route with your school before you plan revision. The [Overview]({{ '/physics/handouts/overview/' | relative_url }}) lists the time, marks and weighting of each paper.

## Which syllabus do the handouts follow?

Every page cites the syllabus topic it works from. Use the [2025–2027 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/664565-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf) for the 2025, 2026 and 2027 series and the [2028–2030 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/744626-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf) from 2028; the 25 topics and the five papers are the same in both.

## Are these official Cambridge materials?

No. Galbadrakh STEM is an independent study site. Questions written for it are original practice questions, not past-paper questions, and the outcome lists on chapter pages are study paraphrases of the syllabus, not quotations. Official past papers and mark schemes are on the [Cambridge International 9702 page](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-international-as-and-a-level-physics-9702/).

## A link opens a section that says “To be added!”

The handouts and question sets are written one chapter at a time. A section that reads “To be added!” has not been written yet; it appears in the same place on every chapter page, so new material is easy to find.
