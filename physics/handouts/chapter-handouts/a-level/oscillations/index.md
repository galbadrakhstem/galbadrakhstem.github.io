## What you need to be able to do

- Use displacement, amplitude, period, frequency, angular frequency and phase difference for oscillations.
- Understand that simple harmonic motion has acceleration proportional to displacement and opposite in direction, $a=-\omega^2x$, with solution $x=x_0\sin\omega t$.
- Use $v=v_0\cos\omega t$ and $v=\pm\omega\sqrt{x_0^2-x^2}$, and interpret graphs of displacement, velocity and acceleration.
- Describe the interchange of kinetic and potential energy, and use $E=\frac{1}{2}m\omega^2x_0^2$ for the total energy.
- Explain light, critical and heavy damping, and resonance as maximum amplitude when a system is forced at its natural frequency.
