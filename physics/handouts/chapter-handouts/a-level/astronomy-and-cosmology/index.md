## What you need to be able to do

- Understand luminosity as the total power radiated by a star, and use the inverse square law $F=L/(4\pi d^2)$ for radiant flux intensity.
- Explain what a standard candle is and how standard candles are used to determine distances to galaxies.
- Use Wien’s displacement law and the Stefan–Boltzmann law $L=4\pi\sigma r^2T^4$ to estimate the surface temperature and radius of a star.
- Use $\Delta\lambda/\lambda\approx\Delta f/f\approx v/c$ for redshift, and explain why redshift suggests that the Universe is expanding.
- Use Hubble’s law $v\approx H_0d$, and explain how it leads to the Big Bang theory.
