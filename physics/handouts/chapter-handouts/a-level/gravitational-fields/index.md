## What you need to be able to do

- Define gravitational field strength as force per unit mass, and represent a field with field lines.
- Treat a uniform sphere as a point mass at its centre, and use Newton’s law of gravitation $F=Gm_1m_2/r^2$.
- Analyse circular orbits by relating the gravitational force to the centripetal acceleration, and describe a geostationary orbit.
- Derive and use $g=GM/r^2$, and explain why $g$ is approximately constant for small changes in height near the Earth’s surface.
- Define gravitational potential, use $\phi=-GM/r$, and use $E_P=-GMm/r$ for two point masses.
