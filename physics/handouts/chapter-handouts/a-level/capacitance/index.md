## What you need to be able to do

- Define capacitance for isolated spherical conductors and parallel-plate capacitors, and use $C=Q/V$.
- Derive and use the formulae for capacitors in series and in parallel.
- Find the energy stored from the area under a potential–charge graph, and use $W=\frac{1}{2}QV=\frac{1}{2}CV^2$.
- Analyse graphs of potential difference, charge and current against time for a capacitor discharging through a resistor.
- Use the time constant $\tau=RC$ and equations of the form $x=x_0e^{-t/RC}$.
