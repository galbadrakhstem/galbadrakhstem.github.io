## What you need to be able to do

- Understand internal energy as the sum of the random kinetic and potential energies of the molecules, determined by the state of the system.
- Relate a rise in temperature to an increase in internal energy.
- Use $W=p\Delta V$ for the work done when a gas changes volume at constant pressure, distinguishing work done by the gas from work done on it.
- Use the first law of thermodynamics $\Delta U=q+W$, where $q$ is the energy transferred to the system by heating and $W$ the work done on the system.
