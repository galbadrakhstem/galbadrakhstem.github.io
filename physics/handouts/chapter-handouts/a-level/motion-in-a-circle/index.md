## What you need to be able to do

- Define the radian and express angular displacement in radians.
- Use angular speed, with $\omega=2\pi/T$ and $v=r\omega$.
- Understand that a force of constant magnitude perpendicular to the direction of motion causes centripetal acceleration and circular motion at constant angular speed.
- Use $a=r\omega^2=v^2/r$ and $F=mr\omega^2=mv^2/r$.
