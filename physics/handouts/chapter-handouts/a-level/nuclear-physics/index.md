## What you need to be able to do

- Use $E=mc^2$, and represent simple nuclear reactions by nuclear equations.
- Define mass defect and binding energy, sketch binding energy per nucleon against nucleon number, and relate it to nuclear fission and fusion.
- Calculate the energy released in nuclear reactions.
- Understand radioactive decay as spontaneous and random, with fluctuations in count rate as evidence.
- Define activity, decay constant and half-life, use $A=\lambda N$ and $\lambda=0.693/t_{1/2}$, and use $x=x_0e^{-\lambda t}$.
