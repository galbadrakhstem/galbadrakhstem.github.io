## What you need to be able to do

- Explain how a piezo-electric transducer generates and detects ultrasound, and how ultrasound gives diagnostic information about internal structures.
- Use specific acoustic impedance $Z=\rho c$ and the intensity reflection coefficient $I_R/I_0=(Z_1-Z_2)^2/(Z_1+Z_2)^2$.
- Use $I=I_0e^{-\mu x}$ for the attenuation of ultrasound and of X-rays.
- Explain how X-rays are produced, find the minimum wavelength from the accelerating potential difference, and explain contrast in X-ray imaging and how CT scanning builds a three-dimensional image.
- Describe PET scanning: a tracer that decays by β⁺ emission, annihilation of a positron with an electron into two gamma-ray photons travelling in opposite directions, and the energy of those photons.
