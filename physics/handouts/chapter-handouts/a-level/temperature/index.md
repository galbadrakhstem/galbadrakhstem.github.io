## What you need to be able to do

- Understand that thermal energy is transferred from a region of higher temperature to one of lower temperature, and that regions at the same temperature are in thermal equilibrium.
- Describe physical properties that vary with temperature and can be used to measure it, and understand that the thermodynamic scale does not depend on any particular substance.
- Convert between kelvin and degrees Celsius using T / K = θ / °C + 273.15, and know that absolute zero is zero kelvin.
- Define and use specific heat capacity and specific latent heat, distinguishing latent heat of fusion from latent heat of vaporisation.
