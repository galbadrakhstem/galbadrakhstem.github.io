## What you need to be able to do

- Use amount of substance in moles and the Avogadro constant.
- Use the equation of state for an ideal gas as $pV=nRT$ and $pV=NkT$, with $k=R/N_A$.
- State the basic assumptions of the kinetic theory of gases.
- Explain how molecular movement causes gas pressure, and derive and use $pV=\frac{1}{3}Nm\langle c^2\rangle$ and the root-mean-square speed.
- Deduce that the average translational kinetic energy of a molecule is $\frac{3}{2}kT$, and use it.
