## What you need to be able to do

- Define electric field strength as force per unit positive charge, use $F=qE$, and represent fields with field lines.
- Use $E=\Delta V/\Delta d$ for the uniform field between charged parallel plates, and describe the motion of charged particles in that field.
- Use Coulomb’s law $F=Q_1Q_2/(4\pi\varepsilon_0r^2)$ and the field of a point charge $E=Q/(4\pi\varepsilon_0r^2)$.
- Define electric potential, relate field strength to potential gradient, and use $V=Q/(4\pi\varepsilon_0r)$.
- Use $E_P=Qq/(4\pi\varepsilon_0r)$ for the electric potential energy of two point charges.
