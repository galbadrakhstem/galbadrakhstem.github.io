## What you need to be able to do

- Use period, frequency and peak value for an alternating current or voltage, with equations of the form $x=x_0\sin\omega t$.
- Know that the mean power in a resistive load is half the maximum power for a sinusoidal alternating current.
- Distinguish root-mean-square and peak values, and use $I_{rms}=I_0/\sqrt{2}$ and $V_{rms}=V_0/\sqrt{2}$.
- Distinguish half-wave and full-wave rectification, using a single diode and a bridge of four diodes.
- Analyse smoothing by a single capacitor, including the effect of the capacitance and the load resistance.
