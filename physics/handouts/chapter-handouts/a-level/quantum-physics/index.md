## What you need to be able to do

- Understand a photon as a quantum of electromagnetic energy, use $E=hf$ and the electronvolt, and use photon momentum $p=E/c$.
- Explain photoelectric emission in terms of threshold frequency, threshold wavelength and work function, and use $hf=\Phi+\frac{1}{2}mv_{max}^2$.
- Explain why the maximum kinetic energy of photoelectrons does not depend on intensity while the photoelectric current is proportional to it.
- Describe the evidence for wave–particle duality, including electron diffraction, and use the de Broglie wavelength $\lambda=h/p$.
- Explain discrete electron energy levels and the formation of emission and absorption line spectra, and use $hf=E_1-E_2$.
