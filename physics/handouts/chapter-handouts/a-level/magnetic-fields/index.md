## What you need to be able to do

- Understand a magnetic field as a field of force produced by moving charges or permanent magnets, and represent it with field lines.
- Use $F=BIL\sin\theta$ with Fleming’s left-hand rule, and define magnetic flux density.
- Use $F=BQv\sin\theta$, describe the circular path of a charged particle in a uniform field and velocity selection, and derive and use the Hall voltage $V_H=BI/(ntq)$.
- Sketch the field patterns of a long straight wire, a flat circular coil and a long solenoid, and explain the forces between current-carrying conductors.
- Define magnetic flux $\Phi=BA$ and flux linkage, and use Faraday’s and Lenz’s laws of electromagnetic induction.
