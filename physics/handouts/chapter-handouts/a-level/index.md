<h1 class="sr-only">A Level — topics 12 to 25</h1>

<div class="simple-row is-handouts">
  <h2 id="a-level">A Level</h2>
  <div>
    <p>The A Level content of 9702: circular motion and gravitation, thermal physics, oscillations, electric and magnetic fields, alternating currents, and modern physics through to medical physics and astronomy. Paper 4 is written on these topics, with the AS Level content assumed.</p>
    <div class="notice">
      <strong>Syllabus:</strong> topics 12–25 are the A Level subject content of Cambridge International AS &amp; A Level Physics 9702, examined in <strong>Paper 4</strong> (A Level Structured Questions); the AS Level topics are assumed knowledge. Examined under the <a href="https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/664565-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf">2025–2027 syllabus</a> or the <a href="https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/744626-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf">2028–2030 syllabus</a> depending on your examination year; each chapter handout links the syllabus it is taken from.
    </div>

    <h3 id="how-to-use">How to use a chapter handout</h3>
    <ol>
      <li>Read the outcomes list and mark any you cannot yet do without notes.</li>
      <li>Work each example yourself before reading the next line of the solution.</li>
      <li>Attempt the self-assessment questions (SA) closed-book, then compare the complete method in SA Sol — relationship, substitution, unit and precision — not only the final answer.</li>
      <li>Do the exam-style questions (ES) and mark them with ES Sol, then finish with past-paper questions on the same topic.</li>
