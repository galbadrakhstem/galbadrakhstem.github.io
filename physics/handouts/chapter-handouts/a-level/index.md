# A Level — topics 12 to 25

<p class="lead">The A Level content of 9702: circular motion and gravitation, thermal physics, oscillations, electric and magnetic fields, alternating currents, and modern physics through to medical physics and astronomy. Paper 4 is written on these topics, with the AS Level content assumed.</p>

<div class="notice">
<strong>Syllabus:</strong> topics 12–25 are the A Level subject content of Cambridge International AS &amp; A Level Physics 9702, examined in <strong>Paper 4</strong> (A Level Structured Questions); the AS Level topics are assumed knowledge. Examined under the <a href="https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/664565-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf">2025–2027 syllabus</a> or the <a href="https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/744626-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf">2028–2030 syllabus</a> depending on your examination year; every topic below links the syllabus it is taken from.
</div>

## How to use a chapter handout

1. Read the outcomes list and mark any you cannot yet do without notes.
2. Work each example yourself before reading the next line of the solution.
3. Attempt the practice questions closed-book, then compare the complete method — relationship, substitution, unit and precision — not only the final answer.
4. Finish with past-paper questions on the same topic.
