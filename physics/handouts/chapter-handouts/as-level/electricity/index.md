## What you need to be able to do

- Understand current as a flow of charge carriers whose charge is quantised, and use $Q=It$ and $I=Anvq$.
- Define potential difference as energy transferred per unit charge, and use $V=W/Q$, $P=VI$, $P=I^2R$ and $P=V^2/R$.
- Define resistance, use $V=IR$, and state Ohm’s law.
- Sketch the current–voltage characteristics of a metallic conductor at constant temperature, a semiconductor diode and a filament lamp, and explain why a lamp’s resistance rises with current.
- Use $R=\rho L/A$, and know that the resistance of a light-dependent resistor falls as light intensity rises and that of a thermistor falls as temperature rises.
