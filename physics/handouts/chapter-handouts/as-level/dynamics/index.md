## What you need to be able to do

- Understand mass as the property that resists a change in motion, and use $F=ma$ knowing that the acceleration is in the direction of the resultant force.
- Define linear momentum and force as the rate of change of momentum, state and apply Newton’s laws, and treat weight as mass times the acceleration of free fall.
- Describe friction and drag qualitatively, including motion in a uniform gravitational field with air resistance and the idea of terminal velocity.
- State the principle of conservation of momentum and apply it to elastic and inelastic interactions in one and two dimensions.
- Recall that in an elastic collision total kinetic energy is conserved and the relative speed of approach equals the relative speed of separation.
