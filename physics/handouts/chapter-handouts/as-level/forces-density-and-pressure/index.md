## What you need to be able to do

- Take the weight of an object as acting at a single point, its centre of gravity.
- Define and apply the moment of a force, and the torque of a couple — a pair of forces that produces rotation only.
- State and apply the principle of moments, and know that a system with no resultant force and no resultant torque is in equilibrium.
- Use a vector triangle to represent coplanar forces in equilibrium.
- Define density and pressure, derive and use $\Delta p=\rho g\Delta h$, explain upthrust as a difference in hydrostatic pressure, and calculate it with $F=\rho gV$.
