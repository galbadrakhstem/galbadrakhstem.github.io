## What you need to be able to do

- Calculate work done as force multiplied by displacement in the direction of the force, and apply the principle of conservation of energy.
- Define efficiency as useful energy output divided by total energy input, and use it to solve problems.
- Define power as work done per unit time, use $P=W/t$, and derive and use $P=Fv$.
- Derive and use $\Delta E_P=mg\Delta h$ for changes of gravitational potential energy in a uniform field.
- Derive $E_K=\frac{1}{2}mv^2$ from the equations of motion, and use it.
