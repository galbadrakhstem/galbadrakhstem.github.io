## What you need to be able to do

- Draw and interpret circuit diagrams using the circuit symbols in section 6 of the syllabus.
- Define electromotive force, distinguish it from potential difference in terms of energy, and explain how internal resistance affects the terminal potential difference.
- Recall Kirchhoff’s first law (conservation of charge) and second law (conservation of energy), and use them to derive and use the formulae for resistors in series and in parallel.
- Use Kirchhoff’s laws to solve simple circuit problems.
- Explain potential dividers, including those using thermistors and light-dependent resistors, the potentiometer for comparing potential differences, and a galvanometer in null methods.
