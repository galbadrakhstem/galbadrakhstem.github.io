## What you need to be able to do

- Define and use distance, displacement, speed, velocity and acceleration, and represent them graphically.
- Find displacement from the area under a velocity–time graph, velocity from the gradient of a displacement–time graph, and acceleration from the gradient of a velocity–time graph.
- Derive the equations for uniformly accelerated motion in a straight line from the definitions of velocity and acceleration, and use them, including free fall without air resistance.
- Describe an experiment to determine the acceleration of free fall using a falling object.
- Explain motion with a uniform velocity in one direction and a uniform acceleration in a perpendicular direction.
