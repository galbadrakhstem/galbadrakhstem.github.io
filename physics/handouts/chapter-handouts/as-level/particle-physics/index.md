## What you need to be able to do

- Infer the existence and small size of the nucleus from the α-particle scattering experiment, and describe the nuclear model of the atom.
- Use proton number, nucleon number, isotopes and nuclide notation, and know that nucleon number and charge are conserved in nuclear processes.
- Describe α-, β⁻-, β⁺- and γ-radiation, write decay equations, and explain why β-particles have a continuous range of energies.
- Understand that an antiparticle has the same mass and opposite charge, and use the unified atomic mass unit.
- Describe the six quark flavours and their charges, the quark composition of protons, neutrons, baryons and mesons, the quark changes in β⁻ and β⁺ decay, and electrons and neutrinos as leptons.
