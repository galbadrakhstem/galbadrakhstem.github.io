# AS Level — topics 1 to 11

<p class="lead">The AS Level content of 9702: measurement and units, mechanics, materials, waves, electricity and particle physics. Papers 1 and 2 are written on these topics, and the A Level papers assume them.</p>

<div class="notice">
<strong>Syllabus:</strong> topics 1–11 are the AS Level subject content of Cambridge International AS &amp; A Level Physics 9702, examined in <strong>Paper 1</strong> (Multiple Choice) and <strong>Paper 2</strong> (AS Level Structured Questions), and assumed knowledge for Paper 4. Examined under the <a href="https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/664565-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf">2025–2027 syllabus</a> or the <a href="https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/744626-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf">2028–2030 syllabus</a> depending on your examination year; every topic below links the syllabus it is taken from.
</div>

## How to use a chapter handout

1. Read the outcomes list and mark any you cannot yet do without notes.
2. Work each example yourself before reading the next line of the solution.
3. Attempt the practice questions closed-book, then compare the complete method — relationship, substitution, unit and precision — not only the final answer.
4. Finish with past-paper questions on the same topic.
