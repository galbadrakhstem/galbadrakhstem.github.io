## What you need to be able to do

- Use displacement, amplitude, phase difference, period, frequency, wavelength and speed; derive and use $v=f\lambda$; and find frequency and amplitude from an oscilloscope trace.
- Understand that a progressive wave transfers energy, and use intensity = power / area and intensity ∝ (amplitude)².
- Compare transverse and longitudinal waves and interpret their graphical representations.
- Use $f_o=f_s v/(v\pm v_s)$ for the observed frequency when a sound source moves relative to a stationary observer.
- State that electromagnetic waves are transverse and travel at the same speed in free space, recall the wavelength ranges of the spectrum (visible light 400–700 nm), and use Malus’s law $I=I_0\cos^2\theta$.
