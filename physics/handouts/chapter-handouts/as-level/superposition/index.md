## What you need to be able to do

- Explain and use the principle of superposition.
- Explain how a stationary wave forms, identify nodes and antinodes, and find the wavelength from their positions, in experiments with microwaves, stretched strings and air columns.
- Explain diffraction and the effect of the gap width relative to the wavelength.
- Understand interference and coherence, the conditions for observing two-source fringes, and use $\lambda=ax/D$ for double-slit interference with light.
- Use $d\sin\theta=n\lambda$ and describe how a diffraction grating is used to determine the wavelength of light.
