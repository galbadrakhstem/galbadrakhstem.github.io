## What you need to be able to do

- Understand that tensile and compressive forces cause deformation, and use the terms load, extension, compression and limit of proportionality.
- Recall and use Hooke’s law and the spring constant $k=F/x$.
- Define and use stress, strain and the Young modulus, and describe an experiment to determine the Young modulus of a metal wire.
- Distinguish elastic deformation, plastic deformation and the elastic limit.
- Find work done from the area under a force–extension graph, and use $E_P=\frac{1}{2}Fx=\frac{1}{2}kx^2$ within the limit of proportionality.
