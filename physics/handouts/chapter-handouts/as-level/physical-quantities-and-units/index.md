## What you need to be able to do

- Treat every physical quantity as a numerical magnitude and a unit, and make reasonable estimates of quantities in the syllabus.
- Recall the SI base quantities and units (kg, m, s, A, K), express derived units in base units, and use base units to check that an equation is homogeneous.
- Use the prefixes from pico to tera for multiples and submultiples of base and derived units.
- Explain systematic errors (including zero errors) and random errors, distinguish precision from accuracy, and combine absolute or percentage uncertainties for a derived quantity.
- Distinguish scalars from vectors, add and subtract coplanar vectors, and resolve a vector into two perpendicular components.
