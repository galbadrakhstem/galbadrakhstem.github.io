## A useful study routine

1. Check that the topic is listed in your current 9709 syllabus.
2. Read the related notes and write down definitions or conditions you need to remember.
3. Work each example yourself before reading the next line.
4. Attempt the practice questions without looking at the solutions; then compare the complete method, not only the final answer.
