  <div>

The chapter handouts follow the 9709 syllabus chapter by chapter: every chapter has the same five links — the handout, self-assessment questions (SA) and their solutions (SA Sol), and exam-style questions (ES) and their solutions (ES Sol) — and the chapters are being written one at a time.</p>

    <h3 id="how-to-prepare">How to prepare</h3>
    <ol>
      <li>Check that the topic is listed in your current 9709 syllabus.</li>
      <li>Read the related notes and write down definitions or conditions you need to remember.</li>
      <li>Work each example yourself before reading the next line.</li>
      <li>Attempt the practice questions without looking at the solutions; then compare the complete method, not only the final answer.</li>
    </ol>
    <ul>
      <li><a href="{{ '/mathematics/exam-overview/' | relative_url }}">Exam overview</a>: check your syllabus year, paper route, and assessment objectives before you plan revision.</li>
