# Mathematics exam overview

Cambridge International AS &amp; A Level Mathematics (9709) assesses mathematical reasoning as well as accurate technique. This page is a study-planning guide; it is not a substitute for the syllabus for your examination year.

## Check your assessment route first

9709 is offered through more than one paper combination. The exact components, syllabus content, weighting, and permitted combinations can vary by examination year and entry route. Before you make a revision timetable:

1. Find the official 9709 syllabus for the year in which you will sit the examination.
2. Confirm with your teacher which paper combination you are entered for.
3. Make a checklist of the syllabus learning outcomes for those components only.
4. Check the examination timetable and any current instructions from Cambridge International.

## What to practise

The syllabus is organised around mathematical content and skills. Depending on your route and level, the material includes pure mathematics and may include applied work such as mechanics or probability and statistics. Use the current syllabus to identify exactly which topics apply to you.

Across topics, practise the following examination habits:

- **Show a connected method.** Write each algebraic transformation and state any formula you use.
- **Use exact values where appropriate.** Keep fractions and surds exact until a question asks for a decimal approximation.
- **Give sufficient accuracy.** Follow the question's instruction; when none is given, use the accuracy convention specified in the syllabus or paper.
- **Interpret the result.** Include units in applied questions and reject values that do not fit the context.
- **Check your answer.** Substitute roots into the original equation or estimate whether a numerical result is reasonable.
