## What a walkthrough contains

<ul class="link-list">
  <li><strong>The paper and its mark scheme.</strong> Every component page links the official Cambridge question paper and mark scheme, and states the syllabus the paper was examined under.</li>
  <li><strong>Question-by-question working.</strong> Each part is solved in full — the method named, every algebraic step written, exact values kept exact.</li>
  <li><strong>Where the marks are.</strong> The method marks (M), accuracy marks (A) and independent marks (B) are tagged against the step that earns them.</li>
  <li><strong>What the examiner saw.</strong> The comments from the examiner report for that series, applied to the question in front of you.</li>
</ul>

<div class="notice">
<strong>Syllabus versions:</strong> 9709 question papers are written to the syllabus in force in that examination series. This site cites the official syllabus documents — <a href="https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/597421-2023-2025-syllabus.pdf">2023–2025</a>, <a href="https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/697427-2026-2027-syllabus.pdf">2026–2027</a> and <a href="https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/744634-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf">2028–2030</a> — so you can check which content applies to the paper you are practising.
</div>
