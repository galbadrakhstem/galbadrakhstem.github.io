## What you need to be able to do

- Understand the terms function, domain, range, one-one function, inverse function and composite function.
- Find the range of a function and the inverse of a one-one function, and know that the graph of the inverse is the reflection of the graph of the function in $y=x$.
- Form and simplify composite functions, and state the domain on which a composite is defined.
- Sketch and use graphs produced by translations, reflections and stretches, including $y=\left|f(x)\right|$ and $y=f(\left|x\right|)$.
- Sketch and use transformations of the graphs of the exponential, logarithmic and trigonometric functions.
