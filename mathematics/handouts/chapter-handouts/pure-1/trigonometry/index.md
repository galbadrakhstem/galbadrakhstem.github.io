## What you need to be able to do

- Sketch and use the graphs of $\sin x$, $\cos x$ and $\tan x$ for angles of any size, in degrees or radians.
- Use the exact values of the sine, cosine and tangent of $30^\circ$, $45^\circ$, $60^\circ$ and related angles.
- Use the identities $\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta\equiv 1$ and $\tan\theta\equiv\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$.
- Solve trigonometric equations in a given interval, including quadratic equations in $\sin\theta$, $\cos\theta$ or $\tan\theta$, and equations that first need an identity.
- Sketch and use graphs such as $y=3\sin x$ and $y=1-\cos 2x$.
