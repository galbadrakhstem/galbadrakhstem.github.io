## What you need to be able to do

- Convert between degrees and radians, and work with angles in radians in exact form.
- Use arc length $s=r\theta$ and sector area $A=\tfrac12 r^2\theta$ in problems.
- Solve problems involving arcs, sectors, segments and perimeters of composite shapes, including triangles inside sectors.
- Use the exact values of trigonometric functions at the common angles, in radians.
