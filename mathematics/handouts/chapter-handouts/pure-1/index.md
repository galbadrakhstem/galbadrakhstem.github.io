  <h2 id="pure-1">Pure 1</h2>
  <div>
    <p>Pure 1 is the foundation of 9709: the algebra, functions, geometry, trigonometry and calculus that every later paper assumes. The chapters below follow the order in which the subject content is usually taught.</p>
    <div class="notice">
      <strong>Syllabus:</strong> Pure 1 is examined in <strong>Paper 1</strong> of Cambridge International AS &amp; A Level Mathematics 9709, subject content 1.1–1.8. Examined under the <a href="https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/697427-2026-2027-syllabus.pdf">syllabus for examination in 2026 and 2027</a>, the <a href="https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/597421-2023-2025-syllabus.pdf">2023–2025 syllabus</a> or the <a href="https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/744634-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf">2028–2030 syllabus</a> depending on your examination year; each chapter handout cites the section it covers.
    </div>

    <h3 id="how-to-use">How to use a chapter handout</h3>
    <ol>
      <li>Read the outcomes list and mark any you cannot yet do without notes.</li>
      <li>Work each example yourself before reading the next line of the solution.</li>
      <li>Attempt the self-assessment questions (SA) closed-book, then compare the complete method in SA Sol, not only the final answer.</li>
