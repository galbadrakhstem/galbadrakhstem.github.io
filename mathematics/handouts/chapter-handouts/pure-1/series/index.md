## What you need to be able to do

- Use the binomial expansion of $(a+b)^n$ for a positive integer $n$, including the notation $\binom{n}{r}$ and the general term.
- Find the term of a binomial expansion containing a given power of the variable.
- Use the arithmetic progression formulae for the $n$th term and the sum of the first $n$ terms.
- Use the geometric progression formulae for the $n$th term and the sum of the first $n$ terms.
- Use the sum to infinity of a convergent geometric progression, and state and use the condition for convergence.
