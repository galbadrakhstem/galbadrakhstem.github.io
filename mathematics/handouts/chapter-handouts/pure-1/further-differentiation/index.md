## What you need to be able to do

- Differentiate products and quotients, and composite functions using the chain rule.
- Use differentiation to solve problems in which two or more quantities change with respect to a third, such as time.
- Set up, and check, a mathematical model whose gradient function is given.
- Know that these techniques are extended in Pure Mathematics 2 §2.4 and Pure Mathematics 3 §3.4, where functions such as $e^x$, $\ln x$ and the trigonometric functions are differentiated.
