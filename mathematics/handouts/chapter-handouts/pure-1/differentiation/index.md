## What you need to be able to do

- Understand the derivative as a gradient and as a rate of change.
- Differentiate $x^n$ for rational $n$, and constant multiples, sums and differences, including composite functions of the form $(ax+b)^n$.
- Apply differentiation to gradients, tangents and normals, and to increasing and decreasing functions.
- Locate stationary points and determine their nature, using the second derivative or an alternative method.
- Solve connected rates of change problems, for example the rate of increase of the area of a circle given the rate of increase of its radius.
