## What you need to be able to do

- Complete the square and use the completed-square form to read off the vertex, the line of symmetry and the minimum or maximum value.
- Use the discriminant $b^2-4ac$ to decide how many real roots a quadratic equation has, and to find restrictions on a parameter.
- Solve quadratic equations, including equations that become quadratic after a substitution.
- Solve quadratic inequalities in one variable and state the solution set correctly.
- Solve a pair of simultaneous equations where one is linear and one is quadratic.
