## What you need to be able to do

- Find the distance between two points and the coordinates of the midpoint of a line segment.
- Find the gradient of a line and use the forms of the equation of a straight line, including the conditions for two lines to be parallel or perpendicular.
- Find the equation of a circle, and find the centre and radius of a circle from its equation, completing the square where necessary.
- Solve problems involving the intersection of a line and a circle, using the discriminant to decide how many times they meet.
- Find the equation of the tangent or the normal to a circle at a given point.
