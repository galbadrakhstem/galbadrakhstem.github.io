## What you need to be able to do

- Find the range and the interquartile range of a set of data, and use them to compare distributions.
- Calculate the variance and standard deviation of a set of data, including data given in a frequency table.
- Use the relationship between the mean and the standard deviation when every value is coded, and undo the coding correctly.
- Interpret a standard deviation in the context of the data, and compare two distributions using a measure of spread as well as an average.
- Recognise how an outlier affects the mean and the standard deviation rather than the median and the interquartile range.
