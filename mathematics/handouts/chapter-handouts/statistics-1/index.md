  <h2 id="statistics-1">Probability &amp; Statistics 1</h2>
  <div>
    <p>Probability &amp; Statistics 1 covers the whole journey from raw data to a probability model: how data is represented, how it is summarised, and how the binomial and normal distributions are used.</p>
    <div class="notice">
      <strong>Syllabus:</strong> Probability &amp; Statistics 1 is examined in <strong>Paper 5</strong> of Cambridge International AS &amp; A Level Mathematics 9709, subject content 5.1–5.5. Examined under the <a href="https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/697427-2026-2027-syllabus.pdf">syllabus for examination in 2026 and 2027</a>, the <a href="https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/597421-2023-2025-syllabus.pdf">2023–2025 syllabus</a> or the <a href="https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/744634-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf">2028–2030 syllabus</a> depending on your examination year; each chapter handout cites the section it covers.
    </div>
