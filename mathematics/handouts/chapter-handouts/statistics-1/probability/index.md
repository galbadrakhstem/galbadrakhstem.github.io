## What you need to be able to do

- Evaluate probabilities of simple and combined events using equally likely outcomes and sample spaces.
- Use the addition and multiplication rules of probability, and the formula for the probability of the union of two events.
- Understand and use the terms mutually exclusive and independent, including the test for independence.
- Use conditional probability, and use tree diagrams and Venn diagrams to represent a problem before calculating.
- Solve problems in which the answer depends on information that arrives in stages.
