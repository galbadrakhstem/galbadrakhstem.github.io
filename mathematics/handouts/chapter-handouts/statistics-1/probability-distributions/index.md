## What you need to be able to do

- Understand the idea of a discrete random variable and construct its probability distribution table.
- Calculate the expectation $E(X)$ and the variance $\operatorname{Var}(X)$ of a discrete random variable, including variables defined by a function of $X$.
- Use $E(aX+b)=aE(X)+b$ and $\operatorname{Var}(aX+b)=a^2\operatorname{Var}(X)$.
- Use a probability distribution to decide whether a game or a process is fair.
