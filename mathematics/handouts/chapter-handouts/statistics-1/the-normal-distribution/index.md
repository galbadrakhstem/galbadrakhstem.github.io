## What you need to be able to do

- Understand the shape and properties of the normal distribution, and why a continuous model is used.
- Standardise a normal variable using $z=\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}$ and use the standard normal distribution tables.
- Find probabilities of the form $P(X<a)$, $P(X>a)$ and $P(a<X<b)$.
- Work backwards from a probability to find an unknown mean, standard deviation or boundary value.
- Recognise when a normal approximation to a binomial or Poisson distribution is appropriate, and apply the continuity correction.
