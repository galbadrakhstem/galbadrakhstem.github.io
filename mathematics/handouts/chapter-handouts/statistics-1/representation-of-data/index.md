## What you need to be able to do

- Understand the terms discrete and continuous data, and qualitative and quantitative variables.
- Select and use suitable tables, charts and diagrams, including stem-and-leaf and back-to-back stem-and-leaf diagrams, for a set of raw data.
- Construct and interpret histograms, including histograms with unequal class widths using frequency density.
- Construct and interpret cumulative frequency graphs, and read medians, quartiles and percentiles from them.
- Construct and interpret box-and-whisker plots, and compare two distributions, including outliers.
