## What you need to be able to do

- Understand the relationship between logarithms and indices, and use the laws of logarithms, excluding change of base.
- Understand the definition and properties of $e^x$ and $\ln x$, including their relationship as inverse functions and their graphs.
- Sketch the graph of $y=e^{kx}$ for positive and negative values of $k$, and use transformations of it.
- Solve equations and inequalities in which the unknown appears in an index, including cases where a substitution such as $u=e^x$ is needed.
- Model exponential growth and decay, including the use of log-linear graphs to estimate constants.
