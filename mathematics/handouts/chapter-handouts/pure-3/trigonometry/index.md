## What you need to be able to do

- Understand the relationship of the secant, cosecant and cotangent functions to cosine, sine and tangent, and use the properties and graphs of all six functions for angles of any magnitude.
- Use the identities $\sec^2\theta\equiv 1+\tan^2\theta$ and $\operatorname{cosec}^2\theta\equiv 1+\cot^2\theta$.
- Use the expansions of $\sin(A\pm B)$, $\cos(A\pm B)$ and $\tan(A\pm B)$, and the double-angle formulae for $\sin 2A$, $\cos 2A$ and $\tan 2A$.
- Express $a\sin\theta+b\cos\theta$ in the forms $R\sin(\theta\pm\alpha)$ and $R\cos(\theta\pm\alpha)$, and solve equations using them.
- Select the identity that fits a problem, and show familiarity with the identities above in solving equations.
