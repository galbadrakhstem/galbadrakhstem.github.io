  <h2 id="pure-3">Pure 3</h2>
  <div>
    <p>Pure 3 is compulsory for A Level. It extends the algebra, logarithms and trigonometry of Pure 1, then adds vectors, differential equations and complex numbers to the calculus.</p>
    <div class="notice">
      <strong>Syllabus:</strong> Pure 3 is examined in <strong>Paper 3</strong> of Cambridge International AS &amp; A Level Mathematics 9709, subject content 3.1–3.9. Examined under the <a href="https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/697427-2026-2027-syllabus.pdf">syllabus for examination in 2026 and 2027</a>, the <a href="https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/597421-2023-2025-syllabus.pdf">2023–2025 syllabus</a> or the <a href="https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/744634-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf">2028–2030 syllabus</a> depending on your examination year; each chapter handout cites the section it covers. Pure 3 assumes the content of Pure 1 throughout, and questions may require both.
    </div>
