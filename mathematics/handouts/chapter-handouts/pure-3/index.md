# Pure 3 — Pure Mathematics 3

<p class="lead">Pure 3 is compulsory for A Level. It extends the algebra, logarithms and trigonometry of Pure 1, then adds vectors, differential equations and complex numbers to the calculus.</p>

<div class="notice">
<strong>Syllabus:</strong> Pure 3 is examined in <strong>Paper 3</strong> of Cambridge International AS &amp; A Level Mathematics 9709, subject content 3.1–3.9. Examined under the <a href="https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/697427-2026-2027-syllabus.pdf">syllabus for examination in 2026 and 2027</a>, the <a href="https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/597421-2023-2025-syllabus.pdf">2023–2025 syllabus</a> or the <a href="https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/744634-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf">2028–2030 syllabus</a> depending on your examination year; every chapter below links the section it covers. Pure 3 assumes the content of Pure 1 throughout, and questions may require both.
</div>

## How to use a chapter handout

1. Read the outcomes list and mark any you cannot yet do without notes.
2. Work each example yourself before reading the next line of the solution.
3. Attempt the practice questions closed-book, then compare the complete method, not only the final answer.
4. Finish with the past-paper questions from the same syllabus section.
