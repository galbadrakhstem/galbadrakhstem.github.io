## What you need to be able to do

- Use vector notation, and find the magnitude and direction of a vector.
- Add and subtract vectors, multiply a vector by a scalar, and work with position vectors.
- Use the scalar product to find the angle between two vectors, and use the condition for two vectors to be perpendicular.
- Find the vector equation of a line, and determine whether two lines are parallel, intersect or are skew.
- Find the point of intersection of two lines, the angle between them, and the perpendicular distance from a point to a line.
