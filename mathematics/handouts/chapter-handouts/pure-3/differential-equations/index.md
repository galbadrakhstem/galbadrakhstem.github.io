## What you need to be able to do

- Formulate a simple statement as a differential equation, including statements about rates of change and proportionality.
- Solve a first order differential equation in which the variables are separable, and find the general solution.
- Use an initial condition or a boundary condition to find the particular solution.
- Sketch members of the family of solution curves and interpret them in the context of the model.
