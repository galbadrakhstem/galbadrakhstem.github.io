## What you need to be able to do

- Extend reverse differentiation to $e^{ax+b}$, $\dfrac{1}{ax+b}$, $\sin(ax+b)$, $\cos(ax+b)$ and $\sec^2(ax+b)$.
- Integrate rational functions by decomposition into partial fractions, restricted to the types of partial fractions in §3.1.
- Integrate using the method of substitution, including the choice of substitution and the change of limits in a definite integral.
- Integrate by parts, including repeated use and the use of the formula for the integral of $\ln x$.
- Use trigonometric identities, such as the double-angle formulae, to integrate $\sin^2 x$ and $\cos^2 x$, and use the trapezium rule to estimate a definite integral and to bound its error.
