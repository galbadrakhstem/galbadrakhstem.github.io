## What you need to be able to do

- Differentiate $e^{ax}$, $\ln x$, $\sin x$, $\cos x$ and $\tan x$, and constant multiples, sums and differences of them.
- Use the product rule and the quotient rule.
- Use the chain rule, including for composite functions of the functions above.
- Differentiate implicitly, including to find the gradient of a curve defined implicitly.
- Differentiate parametrically defined functions, including finding the first and second derivatives of a curve given parametrically.
