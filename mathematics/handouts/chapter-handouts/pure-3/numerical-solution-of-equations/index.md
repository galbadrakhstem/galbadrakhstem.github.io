## What you need to be able to do

- Locate approximately a root of an equation, using graphical considerations and a search for a sign change.
- Use an iterative formula of the form $x_{n+1}=F(x_n)$ to find successive approximations to a root.
- Understand convergence and divergence of an iterative sequence, and interpret the iteration with a staircase or cobweb diagram.
- Explain why an iteration fails to converge, and use the sign-change test to justify an approximation.
