## What you need to be able to do

- Use the modulus function, and solve equations and inequalities involving it.
- Divide a polynomial by a linear or quadratic expression, and understand the terms quotient and remainder.
- Use the factor theorem and the remainder theorem, including to find unknown coefficients.
- Decompose a rational function into partial fractions, including denominators of the form $(ax+b)(cx+d)$, $(ax+b)(cx+d)^2$ and $(ax+b)(x^2+c^2)$.
- Use the binomial expansion of $(1+x)^n$ for rational $n$, and state the set of values of $x$ for which the expansion is valid.
