## What you need to be able to do

- Understand the idea of a complex number and the terms real part, imaginary part, modulus, argument and conjugate.
- Represent complex numbers on an Argand diagram, and carry out addition, subtraction, multiplication and division in Cartesian form.
- Multiply and divide complex numbers in polar form, and use the results $\left|z_1z_2\right|=\left|z_1\right|\left|z_2\right|$ and $\arg(z_1z_2)=\arg z_1+\arg z_2$ with the corresponding results for division.
- Find the square roots of a complex number, showing full details of the working.
- Solve polynomial equations with real coefficients, using the conjugate root property, and sketch loci such as $\left|z-a\right|=r$ and $\arg(z-a)=\theta$.
