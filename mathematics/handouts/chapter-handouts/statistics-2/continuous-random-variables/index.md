## What you need to be able to do

- Understand the concept of a continuous random variable, and use a probability density function.
- Find the cumulative distribution function from a probability density function, and use it to find probabilities.
- Use the relationship between a probability density function and its cumulative distribution function, including differentiation, when the function is given piecewise.
- Find $E(X)$ and $\operatorname{Var}(X)$ of a continuous random variable.
- Find the median, quartiles and other percentiles of a continuous random variable.
