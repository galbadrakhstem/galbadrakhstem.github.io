## What you need to be able to do

- State null and alternative hypotheses, and decide whether a one-tailed or two-tailed test is appropriate.
- Carry out a test of a population mean using a normal distribution, and a test of a proportion or a Poisson mean.
- Find and use a critical region, or compare a $p$-value with the significance level, and state the conclusion in the context of the question.
- Understand Type I and Type II errors, and interpret them in the context of a test.
