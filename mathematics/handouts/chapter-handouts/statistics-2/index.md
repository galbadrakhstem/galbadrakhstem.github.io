  <h2 id="statistics-2">Probability &amp; Statistics 2</h2>
  <div>
    <p>Probability &amp; Statistics 2 assumes Statistics 1 and adds the distributions and inference that an A Level course needs: the Poisson distribution, sampling, and hypothesis tests.</p>
    <div class="notice">
      <strong>Syllabus:</strong> Probability &amp; Statistics 2 is examined in <strong>Paper 6</strong> of Cambridge International AS &amp; A Level Mathematics 9709, subject content 6.1–6.5. Examined under the <a href="https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/697427-2026-2027-syllabus.pdf">syllabus for examination in 2026 and 2027</a>, the <a href="https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/597421-2023-2025-syllabus.pdf">2023–2025 syllabus</a> or the <a href="https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/744634-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf">2028–2030 syllabus</a> depending on your examination year; each chapter handout cites the section it covers. Probability &amp; Statistics 2 assumes the content of Probability &amp; Statistics 1.
    </div>
