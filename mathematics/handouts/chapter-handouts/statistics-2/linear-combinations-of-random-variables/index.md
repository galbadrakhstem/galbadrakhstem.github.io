## What you need to be able to do

- Find the expectation and variance of a linear combination of random variables, including $E(aX+bY)$ and $\operatorname{Var}(aX+bY)$.
- Know and use the conditions under which $\operatorname{Var}(aX+bY)=\operatorname{Var}(aX)+\operatorname{Var}(bY)$.
- Find the expectation and variance of the sum or difference of independent random variables, and of a sum of independent observations of the same variable.
- Solve problems in which two or more independent observations are combined, including their mean.
