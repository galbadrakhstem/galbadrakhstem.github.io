# Step-by-step solutions

<p class="lead">A solution is only useful if you can follow every line. On this site no step is skipped: each one is an algebraic operation, a substitution, or a reason, and nothing is left to a mark scheme to explain.</p>

## How these solutions are written

<ul class="link-list">
  <li><strong>One idea per line.</strong> Each line changes one thing, so you can see exactly where an error would appear.</li>
  <li><strong>The method is named.</strong> Completing the square, the factor theorem, the chain rule — the reason is stated before it is used.</li>
  <li><strong>Exact values are kept exact.</strong> Fractions and surds stay in exact form until the question asks for a decimal.</li>
  <li><strong>Marks are shown.</strong> Where a question has an illustrative mark scheme, the M and A marks are tagged against the step that earns them.</li>
  <li><strong>The answer is checked.</strong> Substitution back into the original equation, or a statement of why the value is reasonable.</li>
</ul>
