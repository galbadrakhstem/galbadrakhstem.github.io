# Last-minute prep

<p class="lead">The last week is not the time to learn a new topic. It is the time to make sure the material you already know survives the pressure of the examination hall.</p>

## The five-day plan

<div class="table-wrap">
<table>
  <thead>
    <tr><th>Day</th><th>What to do</th><th>Why</th></tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>Five days before</td><td>Write out, from memory, the formulae for the paper you are sitting. Then check them against the syllabus.</td><td>You find the gaps while there is still time to close them.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Four days before</td><td>One full past paper under timed conditions. Mark it yourself with the official mark scheme.</td><td>Timing and mark allocation are examined as much as mathematics.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Three days before</td><td>Re-do every question you got wrong. Write down what the error actually was.</td><td>Most lost marks come from a small number of repeated slips.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Two days before</td><td>Skim each chapter handout and read only the boxes marked idea, pitfall and key takeaway.</td><td>Consolidation, not new content.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>The day before</td><td>One or two short questions, then stop. Check the start time, the duration, and the stationery you are allowed.</td><td>Arrive rested and clear about the practical details.</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
</div>

## Conditions people forget

<ul class="link-list">
  <li><strong>Discriminant:</strong> the number of real roots is decided by the sign of $b^2-4ac$ — positive gives two, zero gives one, negative gives none — and any restriction on a parameter must be stated.</li>
  <li><strong>Logarithms:</strong> $x$ must be positive, so solutions of $\ln$ equations have to be checked before they are accepted.</li>
  <li><strong>Radian measure:</strong> calculus with trigonometric functions only works in radians, and an angle in degrees must be converted.</li>
  <li><strong>Normal distribution:</strong> state the distribution you standardise to, and whether the probability is a tail or between two values.</li>
  <li><strong>Statistics:</strong> a hypothesis test needs the null hypothesis, the alternative, the distribution assumed, and a comparison with the significance level.</li>
</ul>
