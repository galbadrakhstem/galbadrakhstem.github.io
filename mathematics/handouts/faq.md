# Frequently asked questions

<p class="lead">Short answers about how the Mathematics 9709 handouts are organised and how to use them.</p>

## What do Handout, SA, SA Sol, ES and ES Sol mean?

Every chapter has the same five links. **Handout** opens the chapter handout: the syllabus reference, what you need to be able to do, notes and worked examples. **SA** is a set of self-assessment questions and **SA Sol** their full solutions; **ES** is a set of exam-style questions and **ES Sol** their full solutions.

## Which papers do the chapter handouts cover?

Four: Pure Mathematics 1 (Paper 1), Probability &amp; Statistics 1 (Paper 5), Pure Mathematics 3 (Paper 3) and Probability &amp; Statistics 2 (Paper 6). The past-paper walkthroughs also cover Pure Mathematics 2 (Paper 2) and Mechanics (Paper 4).

## Which papers do I sit?

Papers 1 and 3 are compulsory for A Level. Papers 4 and 6 cannot be combined with each other, and Paper 2 and Paper 3 are alternatives, so confirm your own route with your school before you plan revision. The [Overview]({{ '/mathematics/handouts/overview/' | relative_url }}) lists the time, marks and weighting of each paper.

## Which syllabus do the handouts follow?

Every page cites the syllabus section it works from. Use the [2026–2027 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/697427-2026-2027-syllabus.pdf) for the 2026 and 2027 series, the [2028–2030 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/744634-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf) from 2028, and the [2023–2025 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/597421-2023-2025-syllabus.pdf) with papers sat between 2023 and 2025. The [notation list](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/420009-mathematics-notation-list-.pdf) shows the notation you are expected to read and write.

## Are these official Cambridge materials?

No. Galbadrakh STEM is an independent study site. Questions written for it are original practice questions, not past-paper questions, and the outcome lists on chapter pages are study paraphrases of the syllabus, not quotations. Official past papers and mark schemes are on the [Cambridge International 9709 page](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-international-as-and-a-level-mathematics-9709/).

## A link opens a section that says “To be added!”

The handouts and question sets are written one chapter at a time. A section that reads “To be added!” has not been written yet; it appears in the same place on every chapter page, so new material is easy to find.
