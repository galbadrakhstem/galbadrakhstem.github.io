# Overview

<p class="lead">Cambridge International AS &amp; A Level Mathematics is syllabus code 9709. It is examined in six components; your qualification depends on which of them you sit.</p>

Papers 1 and 3 are compulsory for A Level. Papers 4 and 6 cannot be combined with each other, and Paper 2 and Paper 3 are alternatives, so confirm your own route with your school before you plan revision.

## How to use these handouts

1. Read the overview of the paper you are sitting, then check which chapters it contains.
2. Work through the chapter handouts in order; each one states the syllabus section it covers.
3. Use the step-by-step solutions to compare method, not just answers.
4. Finish with past-paper walkthroughs under timed conditions, then re-read the mark scheme.
