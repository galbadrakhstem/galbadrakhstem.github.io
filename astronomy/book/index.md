# Book — Cosmic Calculations: Orbital Mechanics

<p class="lead">First volume of a series for students who, like me, could not find books that were easy to understand. Written to be the resource I wish I had.</p>

<div class="simple-row">
  <h2>About the Book</h2>
  <div>
    <p><em>Cosmic Calculations: Orbital Mechanics</em> starts from zero and builds step-by-step: Newton's laws, gravitation, circular motion, Kepler's laws, energy, and orbital transfers. Every derivation shows every step, with units and reasoning explicit — same philosophy as my Cambridge handouts.</p>
    <p>Motivation: when I started astronomy, most books jumped over the calculations. I wanted a book where you can see what the examiner (or the sky) is asking, what information you have, which method to use, and how the working gets to the answer without needing AI or a second website.</p>
  </div>
</div>
