# Club — Physics & Astronomy Club

<p class="lead">Founded 14 Nov 2024 at Mongol Aspiration International School (Mongol Temuulel) to give students the starting point that is often missing.</p>

<div class="simple-row">
  <h2>Purpose</h2>
  <div>
    <p>A community where curiosity is welcome, questions have room to develop, and a student who knows nothing about astrophysics can grow step by step toward something real. The aim is to help students see the sky not just as a backdrop, but as a system whose rules can be learned.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="simple-row">
  <h2>Teaching</h2>
  <div>
    <p>Orbital mechanics is the core topic. Taught throughout 9th and 10th grade, returning to same material with new students, new questions and new gaps to bridge. A-level physics content is explained with intuitive methods for high-school students.</p>
    <p>In July 2026 the founder was invited to teach the astronomy component to students preparing for the International Earth Science Olympiad.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="simple-row">
  <h2>Results</h2>
  <div>
    <ul>
      <li>2025–2026: Club members — 4 medals middle level, 2 medals senior level at National Astronomy Olympiad</li>
      <li>Member D. Dolgion — prize winner at International Astronomy Olympiad</li>
      <li>Members delivered research presentations, including one on orbital resonance</li>
      <li>6 students from school won medals at National Olympiad (2024–2025 cycle)</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>
