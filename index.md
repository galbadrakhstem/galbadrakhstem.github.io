<div class="simple-row">
  <h2>Introduction</h2>
  <div>
    <p>I am a Cambridge International AS and A Level student, and I study Mathematics, Physics and Computer Science. This site is where I put my main work: handouts, step-by-step solutions, self-assessment questions and exam-style questions for these subjects.</p>
    <p>Every solution here shows every step. You can see what the examiner is looking for, which information the question gives you, which method to use, and how the working gets to the answer. You should not need an AI tool or a second website to understand a question.</p>
    <p>I started this because mark schemes show what counts as correct, but not where the answer comes from. I wanted to fill that gap.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="simple-row">
  <h2>Astronomy and the Club</h2>
  <div>
    <p>My second focus is astronomy. I founded the Physics &amp; Astronomy Club at Mongol Aspiration International School on 14 November 2024, so that students who want to learn astronomy have a place to start. <a href="{{ '/club/' | relative_url }}">Read about the club</a>.</p>
    <p>I also wrote <em>Cosmic Calculations: Orbital Mechanics</em>, the first volume of a series for students who, like me, could not find books that were easy to understand. I wrote it to be the resource I wish I had.</p>
    <p>— Galbadrakh Ulziibold</p>
